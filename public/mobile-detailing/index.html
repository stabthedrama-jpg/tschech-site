How monthly care works

  • Start with a Full Reset at the regular first-clean price. We confirm whether your vehicle is ready for maintenance.
  • Reserve one maintenance appointment about every 4 weeks for the same vehicle.
  • Pay after each completed visit. No enrollment fee, no annual contract and no charge for a visit we cannot provide.
  • Maintenance covers normal daily use. New heavy messes, pet hair and extraction are quoted separately.

Monthly care is a lighter service than the Full Reset. Longer gaps or substantial new buildup may need another reset at the published price, agreed before work begins. Winter visits depend on a suitable heated workspace; outdoor exterior work is weather permitting.

Before your appointment
A few good things to know.
Will this remove every stain or scratch?
Our resets clean normal everyday dirt, with light spot treatment included. Deep fabric extraction, heavy pet hair and heavy salt buildup cost extra. Some stains are permanent. These packages do not remove paint scratches or include polishing.
Can you detail my car during a Wisconsin winter?
Interior appointments need a suitable heated workspace in cold weather. Exterior work depends on safe temperatures and site conditions. If the full service cannot be completed, we agree on a revised service and price or reschedule. Monthly visits that cannot be provided are not billed.
Do I need to be home the whole time?
You need to arrange vehicle and utility access and be available for the final walk-through. Tell us if you need a different handoff arrangement. We’ll agree on it before the appointment.
Can I cancel monthly care or reschedule?
Yes. Email before the next visit to cancel monthly care. Please give 24 hours’ notice to reschedule when possible. Monthly visits are billed only after completion; any extra cleaning is approved first.
What about child seats and personal belongings?
Please remove valuables, loose belongings and child seats before we arrive. We do not remove or reinstall child seats. We can clean accessible areas around a seat that stays installed.
