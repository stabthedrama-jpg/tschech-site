What is TSCHECH Labs?

A structure for starting.

Not every worthwhile idea begins with a business plan. Sometimes it begins with a nurse noticing a gap, a hungry factory worker wanting a better lunch, or a person in recovery wanting to make something that carries weight.

TSCHECH Labs gives those beginnings a name, a shared home, and permission to grow before they have everything figured out.

“Is this all one company?”

Sort of. Think less conglomerate, more community garden: separate plots, shared tools, and a suspicious number of projects started because somebody said, “You know what would be cool?”
