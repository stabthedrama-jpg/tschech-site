The origin story

Why make a coin by hand?

I’m Taylor, and I’m in recovery. Milestones matter to me—not because a coin does the work, but because sometimes you need something solid in your hand to remind you of the work you’ve already done.

I want to learn how to melt, cast, and hand-press silver medallions that celebrate the days people fought for: a sober anniversary, a fresh start, a promise kept, or any moment that deserves more than a date on a calendar.

Puddle & Press is the record of me learning to make them. Slowly, honestly, and from scratch.

— Taylor
The beautifully unofficial process

From puddle
to keepsake.

The long-term goal is simple. The route there involves heat, metal, tools, practice, and a healthy respect for everything I do not know yet.

01

Melt

Start with .999 silver and enough heat to turn something precious into a bright, unruly puddle.

02

Shape

Pour, cast, trim, and coax the metal toward a blank—keeping the evidence that human hands were involved.

03

Press

Strike a design with weight and meaning. Finish it, learn from it, and make the next one better.

The point of the project

Meaning over perfection.

This is not a polished mint pretending it appeared overnight. It is a public learning project built around honest craft and the belief that progress is worth marking.

✦Handmade character, not machine-perfect sameness.
✦Real silver, clear markings, and honest materials.
✦Medallions designed to honor recovery and meaningful human milestones.
Currently: filling the workbench.

Researching tools, collecting equipment, sketching marks, and learning what it takes to make the first honest round.

There is no launch date.
That is part of the charm.
