The idea

Put idle capacity to work.

Starting a physical business can mean paying for tools, space, and specialized help before you know if customers want what you make. Meanwhile, useful equipment across the Valley sits idle between jobs. TSCHECH Pool aims to connect those two sides for small, practical trials.

Equipment

Space

Locate kitchen time, workshop space, or room to stage a short project.
