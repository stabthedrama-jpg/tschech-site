Rooted in independence

More safe days at home.

Deep Roots is developing a nurse-led service for older adults who want to remain safely and independently at home — and for families who want a clearer picture before a small problem becomes a crisis.

Help us design it

The idea

Clinical eyes. Familiar context. Practical follow-through.

Know the baseline

Establish a real-world picture of health, medications, function, mobility, safety, support, and personal goals.

Watch for change

Focus recurring visits on what is different from the person’s usual baseline rather than repeating the same checklist.

Act earlier

Turn concerns into practical next steps: monitor, educate, address, or connect with the right clinician or community resource.

Help us build the right thing

We want criticism before we want customers.

We’re collecting feedback from older adults and families, as well as nurses, clinicians, and community professionals. These surveys are research and service-design tools, not a sales commitment.
